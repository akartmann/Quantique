## Carried into the spines

- Desktop-web first with tablet readiness and touch/keyboard equivalence.
- Standards-first semantic control surfaces, non-drag alternatives, no canvas-only critical interactions, and non-colour-only evidence.
- Local-only state, offline resilience, no-login/no-tracking learning path, and source/rights transparency.
- Calm, bounded inquiry rather than a freeform sandbox or gamified performance loop.

## Not duplicated

- Market comparisons, licensing, regulation, data protection, engine-selection rationale, and release strategy remain in the research report.

## Open UX implications

- Research recommends clear focus, touch target, motion, contrast, and screen-reader criteria but does not select product-specific thresholds or visual direction.
