# Development Epics — Fracture of Certainty

## Delivery Sequence

| # | Epic | Outcome | Exit gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Investigation foundation | Accessible apparatus controls, notebook, theory board, local progress, source labels, and peer-review loop. | Equivalent keyboard, pointer, and touch completion of a representative interaction. |
| 2 | Young validation slice | A 20–30 minute double-slit case with two controls, measurement comparison, sourced debrief, and recognition. | Learner, educator, scholarly, rights-ledger, Phaser-accessibility, performance, and offline-reload gates pass. |
| 3 | Reusable case hardening | A repeatable case-authoring framework proven through a second-case spike. | A second case can be authored without duplicating core behavior. |
| 4 | Morley–Miller ether-drift tutorial | A full first campaign case teaching thermal confounds, resets, and revision. | Young's learner, educator, historical-rigor, accessibility, and technical gates already pass; the 1907 report and 1887 source context are reviewed. |
| 5 | Hafele–Keating relativity case | A full case centred on divergent atomic clocks and competing interpretations. | 1972 prediction/observation sources, error-budget interaction, accessible model, and reviewer approval complete. |
| 6 | Hensen et al. entanglement case | A full final case balancing setup reliability, finite statistics, and critique. | 2015 source review, an accessible CHSH interaction, and reviewer approval complete. |
| 7 | Classroom release readiness | Educator materials, rights ledger audit, accessibility verification, and deployment readiness. | Five educator reviews plus final source/rights and accessibility sign-off. |

## High-Level Stories

### Epic 1 — Investigation foundation

- As a player, I can adjust any lab control using pointer, touch, or keyboard with the same outcome.
- As a player, I can save, compare, reset, and export my observations.
- As a player, I can distinguish a primary artifact, reconstruction, interpretation, and fiction.
- As a player, I can revise a peer-reviewed conclusion without losing my decision history.

### Epic 2 — Young validation slice

- As a player, I can inspect contextual artifacts and make a prediction before using the apparatus.
- As a player, I can set slit spacing and screen distance, then observe and record fringe spacing.
- As a player, I can compare two runs and use them in a conclusion that states a limit.
- As a player, I receive recognition for replication, curiosity, and source discipline—not speed.
- As an educator, I receive a concise context sheet, learning objective, run time, prerequisites, and debrief before a public validation release.

### Epic 3 — Reusable case hardening

- As a content author, I can define a case's controls, evidence, feedback, and debrief without rebuilding the core loop.
- As a reviewer, I can audit each claim and asset from a case's source/rights ledger.

### Epics 4–6 — Campaign cases

- As a player, I encounter a fresh fictional team and laboratory without losing the established inquiry language.
- As a player, I solve each case through authored evidence and experiments, while historical outcomes remain transparent and fact-bound.
- As an educator, I can use each case as a self-contained activity with context, duration, prerequisite, and debrief.
- As a player in the Morley–Miller case, I distinguish time-dependent thermal drift from an orientation-dependent ether signal and issue a bounded conclusion.
- As a player in the Hafele–Keating case, I separate velocity and altitude contributions, weigh an independent skeptic's error-budget critique against the east/west results, and state what would change my conclusion.
- As a player in the Delft case, I make detector completeness, random-setting, timing, and run-count trade-offs legible, then reject or retain the local-realist null only to the strength supported by the evidence.

### Epic 7 — Classroom release readiness

- As an educator, I can deploy the game by direct URL without account creation or tracking.
- As an accessibility reviewer, I can verify equivalent input paths and non-visual alternatives for essential scientific information.
