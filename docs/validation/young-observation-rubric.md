# Young observation rubric

Complete this rubric by direct facilitator observation during a consented moderated session. Do not derive either measure from application state, events, IndexedDB, exports, Playwright, telemetry, or analytics.

| Field | Binary definition |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded-evidence explanation | **Yes** only when the participant, in their own words, cites a recorded observation or an apparatus setting while explaining their conclusion. **No** when neither is cited. |
| Beyond-minimum variable test | **Yes** only when the participant voluntarily tests at least one apparatus variable beyond the minimum guided path. **No** when they do not. |

## Calculation

For each measure, the **numerator** is the count of included participants with a **Yes** observation. The **denominator** is the count of included consented sessions with an observable outcome for that measure. Exclude a session only when consent is withdrawn, the session is abandoned before an observable outcome, or a documented technical failure prevents the observation; record the exclusion count and reason category in facilitator-held notes.

`percentage = (numerator / denominator) × 100`

Each target passes only at **>= 60%**. Record the calculation date and validation lead in `young-validation-aggregate-template.md`. A denominator of zero fails the gate. These observations are facilitator evidence, not product facts.
